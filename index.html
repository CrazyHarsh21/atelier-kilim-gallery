CENTURY-OLD CRAFTSMANSHIP

THE ALCHEMY OF TIME & VEGETABLE DYES

True antique kilims possess an optical vibration impossible to replicate in modern textiles. The wool was washed in snow-fed mountain rivers, hand-carded with iron combs, and colored with wild botanicals gathered across Anatolian high plateaus.

Wild Madder (Rubia)

Root aged 3–5 years for deep terracotta and brick red hues that never run or fade.

Fermented Woad & Indigo

Oxygenated fermentation baths creating eternal lapis, midnight, and sky blues.

Dried Wild Saffron & Reseda

Sun-warmed golds and warm ochres derived from yellow weld and crocus stamens.

Green Walnut Rind

Naturally tannin-rich husks stewed in copper cauldrons for tobacco browns and char.

SACRED TRIBAL SEMIOTICS

THE LANGUAGE OF MOTIFS

Nomadic weavers did not follow written blueprints. They wove their prayers, seasonal migrations, and ancestral protections into each geometric talisman.

Elibelinde

Mother Goddess

Hands-on-hips archetype representing maternal power, abundant harvest, family harmony, and endless vitality.

Koçboynuzu

Ram's Horn

Symbol of bravery, physical endurance against high-altitude winter blizzards, paternal protection, and lineage strength.

Göz

Eye of Protection

Concentric diamond amulet warding off the envious eye, negative spirits, and safeguarding peaceful nomadic dwellings.

Su Yolu

Running Water

Honoring pure glacial river streams that sustain life, pastures, and nomadic encampments on the plateau.

Sandıklı

The Dowry Chest

Symbol of marital loyalty, sacred hopes, and private heirloom treasures prepared by a young weaver for her union.

Bukağı

Ties of Union

Fetters of unbroken devotion, uniting two nomadic families through generations of kinship and mutual alliance.
